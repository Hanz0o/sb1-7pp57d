For IT Solutions

A complete identity for Shammy — a bespoke IT studio building applications, web platforms and UI/UX. The system runs on one idea: Break the Pattern · Dominate the Market.

The idea

One element refuses to conform and pulls ahead of the crowd — the standout node, a tidy grid where a single orange dot breaks rank.

Colour

Deep green for depth & trust, vibrant orange for the breakout, on a warm cream base.

Type

Lato (Latin) + Cairo (Arabic) — the system runs bilingual EN / AR throughout.

Note — Led with dark green + vibrant orange per the brief. Explore the logo directions, the full system and every asset below.
